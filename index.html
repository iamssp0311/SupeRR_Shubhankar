How the ratings are built

Pressure. Leverage Index = the expected absolute swing in win probability from the next ball, computed before it is bowled from the outcome mix of that exact match state, divided by the all-ball average (×1.25 in knockouts). The top 20% of balls are "pressure"; they carry of all win-probability movement.

Win probability. A calibrated chase model (logistic with smooth interactions, isotonic-corrected) and a first-innings model built from re-estimated DLS-style resource curves so the two agree at the innings break; everything scored out-of-fold by match.

Ball value. Runs above an average player's expectation in the identical situation (gradient-boosted, no player identity), with every wicket priced in runs at that moment from the win-probability model.

Ratings. Ridge regression per component (batting: scoring, survival; bowling: economy, wickets) with player baseline + pressure lift and the same terms for the opponent, recency- and competition-weighted, penalties chosen by match-grouped cross-validation.

Ranking. 200 match-level bootstraps; ranking score = 20th percentile ("the level we are 80% sure he clears"). Eligibility: ≥150 pressure balls (bowlers ≥180) and ≥15 pressure innings, active in 2025–26.

Validation. 2022–24 ratings predict 2025–26 pressure value at r = (bowlers) and (batters); batting average under pressure manages . Full detail in the methodology report.
